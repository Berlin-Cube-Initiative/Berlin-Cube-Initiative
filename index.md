## Registration and payment

1. **Register your team** with the form above. One person per team, the captain, fills it in. Registration closes on **21 November 2026**. If we are full, you go on the waitlist. By registering, you agree to the event rules and the privacy notice.
2. **Every player needs a GuildGate account.** We run the tournament there.
3. There are **3 pricing tiers** for each player: 10 EUR regular entry, 5 EUR with a student discount and 15 EUR supporter tier if you want to support this and our next event.
4. The **captain pays once for the whole team in cash**, either on the event date or in advance at a cube meetup in Berlin.

## Captains

Each team has a captain. The captain makes sure that:

- the whole team has the event information,
- everyone has a GuildGate account and is enrolled in the event,
- everyone arrives on time and the team is ready for each draft and match,
- any problems affecting the team are reported to the organizers early.

## Everyone is welcome

This event is for everyone, whatever your gender, sexuality, background, age, ability or experience level. Come as you are, play at your level and expect to be treated with respect.

**House rules:** be respectful, use the name and pronouns people give you, keep trash talk friendly and help newcomers. Harassment, discrimination and unwanted attention are not tolerated, at the venue or on Discord.

**If something happened,** or you feel unsafe or uncomfortable, tell any organizer or email berlin.cube.initiative@gmail.com. We take every report seriously and handle it discreetly. We may talk to the people involved, change a seat or pairing, or ask someone to leave without a refund.

Need anything to make the weekend work for you? Email us and we will do our best.
  

## ❓ FAQ

**I don't have a team. Is that a problem?**
Not at all! Join our Discord and head to the team-finding channel to meet other players looking for teammates: [Find a team on Discord](https://discord.gg/gcKY4UhXcK){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. Once your team of 3 is set, one person registers as captain.

**What if I need to cancel?**
Please email us as soon as possible at berlin.cube.initiative@gmail.com. Your spot can then go to a team on the waitlist.

**What format is this?***

- **4 rounds, 1 round = 1 draft.** Rounds 1 to 3 are Swiss, round 4 is the final round.
- **Team draft:** two teams per pod. Every player plays all three opponents, so each draft has 9 matches. The first team to win 5 matches wins the draft.
- **All matches are best of three.**
- **Cubes:** All drafts are done with the [PowerLSV Cube](https://cubecobra.com/cube/list/PowerLSV).
- For everything else, see [tournament rules]({{ '/rules/' | relative_url }}).

**What prizes can I win?**
There will be no cash prizes. Instead, expect real, physical prizes for the top 3 teams and a little souvenir for every player. On top of that, there will be a raffle and a set of achievements or event goodies.

**Is food provided?**
No. We don't provide food. There is a 45 minute lunch break each day, so plan your own lunch. Snacks and drinks to share are welcome.

**What should I bring?**
We provide the cubes, sleeves and some dice. Please bring your own playmat, a way to track life total and dice. You also need a phone or other device with your GuildGate account.

**Where are the rules?**
See the [tournament rules]({{ '/rules/' | relative_url }}).

**Who do I ask if something is unclear?**
Ask in our [event channel on Discord](https://discord.gg/X9w7s2Vv9J){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, or email us at berlin.cube.initiative@gmail.com.
