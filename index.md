## 🎲 Format

- **4 rounds. 1 round = 1 draft.
- ** Rounds 1 to 3 are Swiss, round 4 is the final round.
- **Team draft:** two teams per pod. Every player plays all three opponents, so each draft has 9 matches. The first team to win 5 matches wins the draft.
- **All matches are best of three.**
- **Cubes:** LSV Power Max Cube and LSV Cube.
- Every round starts with about 80 minutes of drafting and deck building, followed by the matches.

### 🔀 Pairings

| Round | Pairings |
|---|---|
| 1 | Random or seeded |
| 2 | By record: winners against winners, losers against losers |
| 3 | By record, no rematches where possible |
| 4 | Final: 1st vs 2nd. Match for 3rd place: 3rd vs 4th. |

After round 3, one team will be at 3-0, three at 2-1, three at 1-2 and one at 0-3. The top four teams play the final round.

### ⚖️ Tiebreakers

If teams have the same number of draft wins, the order is:

1. Draft wins
2. Total individual match wins in rounds 1 to 3
3. Game wins

## 👑 Captains

Each team has a captain. The captain makes sure that:

- the whole team has the event information,
- everyone has a GuildGate account and is enrolled in the event,
- everyone arrives on time and the team is ready for each draft and match,
- any problems affecting the team are reported to the organizers early.
