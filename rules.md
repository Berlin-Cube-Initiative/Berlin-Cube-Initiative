# 📖 Tournament rules

[← Back to the event page]({{ '/' | relative_url }})

## ⚔️ Draft and matches

- The Format is [Team Draft]([url](https://magic.wizards.com/en/formats/team-booster-draft)).
- Two teams per pod. Every player plays all three opponents, so a draft has 9 matches.
- The first team to win 5 matches wins the draft.
- All matches are best of three.
- Please play all 9 matches in every draft, even after a team has reached 5 wins. Individual match wins count for the tiebreakers.

## 🤝 Rules enforcement

This is a friendly community event, so there are no official judges. We play at **Regular Rules Enforcement Level**.

If a rules question comes up that nobody on your team can answer, the organizers can help with a ruling based on the rules information available online, as long as they are not your opponents. If not, someone from another table may be able to help.

## 📋 Open decklists

We use open decklists. Before each match, players exchange decklists with their opponents. You may look at your opponent's list between games and return it after the match. Please write card names clearly and in English, since players have different native languages.

## 🗣️ Team communication during matches

Players may ask their teammates for advice or rules help, but please keep it limited so the round does not slow down.

**Only help when asked.** Don't interrupt a teammate's game, point out plays, suggest actions or give advice nobody asked for. In short: ask if you need help, answer if you are asked, otherwise let your teammates play their own games.

Questions should be specific, brief, used sparingly and answered quickly. Good examples:

- "Should I mulligan?"
- "A or B?"
- "What does this card do?"
- "How does this interaction work?"

They are not meant to turn into a team discussion during a match. Normally, ask only your adjacent teammate. The exception is a rules question that this teammate cannot answer well.

## 🛠️ Issues

Tell an organizer early about any logistical or technical problem, for example with GuildGate or a missing player.
